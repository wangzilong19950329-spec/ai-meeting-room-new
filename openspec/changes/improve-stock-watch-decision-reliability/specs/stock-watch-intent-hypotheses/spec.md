## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Intent remains a hypothesis without account truth
Intent research SHALL preserve model-audit and server-profile provenance and competing explanations. Price or flow outcomes MUST NOT be relabeled as confirmed real-account accumulation or distribution accuracy.

#### Scenario: Distribution warning precedes a decline
- **WHEN** a model distribution phrase is followed by the defined price decline
- **THEN** it may support the separately defined risk warning outcome but does not confirm real-account distribution intent

### Requirement: Registered observable predictions and counterevidence
Intent research SHALL use finite registered metric, operator and window templates with explicit positive predictions, invalidation conditions and missing-data handling. Model output MUST NOT execute arbitrary expressions or modify test verdicts.

#### Scenario: Competing explanations for inflow during decline
- **WHEN** the same facts permit absorption and passive-buying explanations
- **THEN** the research projection keeps both explanations and only evaluates their registered observable predictions

### Requirement: No self-validation and precise supported semantics
The fact owner MUST evaluate existing tests before creating new tests from the same observation. SUPPORTED SHALL be presented with its specific predicate and observation coverage, not as proof of hidden intent or as equivalent to merely avoiding all counterevidence in a positive-prediction test.

#### Scenario: No invalidation in a legacy test
- **WHEN** a legacy mature test receives SUPPORTED because the specified invalidation did not fire
- **THEN** the report describes that legacy program-test result without upgrading it to a positively confirmed intent

### Requirement: Independent intent progress and eligible history
Intent research SHALL proceed independently of downside-risk effectiveness, with its own observable-prediction, falsification, unresolved and coverage statistics. Historical prompt inclusion MUST retain eligibility and compatible-version gates.

#### Scenario: Risk protocol has not unblinded
- **WHEN** downside-risk effectiveness remains unknown
- **THEN** eligible intent predictions and counterevidence can still be collected without enabling unsupported intent claims or changing the risk protocol
